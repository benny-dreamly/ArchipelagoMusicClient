v5.3.0 is an incremental update with a new queueing feature.

# What's New

- Queue Unplayed: right-click an album while connected to Archipelago for a new "Queue Unplayed" action that queues only the songs not yet played in your current slot (using the server-synced played-song tracking). "Queue All Songs" still queues everything for re-listens.
